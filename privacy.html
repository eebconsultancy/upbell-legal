Privacy Policy

Upbell: Mission Alarm Clock

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

1. Introduction

EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates Upbell: Mission Alarm Clock ("Upbell" or the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

Upbell is designed to work primarily on your device. It does not require account registration and does not operate an advertising or user-tracking system.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Upbell may store the following information locally to provide its features:

  • Alarm times, schedules, labels, sounds, and snooze preferences.
  • Wake-up mission selections, configurations, and completion records.
  • Morning routines, habits, and related progress.
  • Sleep-session information, possible snoring events, and sleep reports.
  • App settings and preferences.
  • Reference information you provide to configure supported wake-up missions.

This information is used to operate the App's features and is not transmitted to servers operated by us.

3. Camera Access

With your permission, Upbell uses your device's camera for supported wake-up missions, including scanning a barcode or QR code and configuring or completing a photo-based mission.

Camera access is used for these functions on your device. Upbell does not upload camera images or scanned content to our servers.

4. Microphone and Sleep Features

With your permission, Upbell may access the microphone during an active sleep-tracking session to analyze sounds that may indicate snoring.

Sleep analysis is performed on your device. Upbell does not transmit microphone recordings or sleep analysis information to our servers.

Sleep-related observations are estimates and are not medical measurements or diagnoses.

5. Motion and Activity Information

Certain wake-up missions may use device motion or activity information to detect actions such as shaking, walking, or squatting.

This information is processed to determine whether a selected mission has been completed. It is not transmitted to our servers.

6. Alarms and Notifications

Upbell uses Apple's alarm and notification technologies to schedule alarms, display alerts, and support wake-up reminders.

Alarm permissions and notification settings are controlled by iOS. You can review or change these permissions in your device's Settings.

7. Data Collection, Tracking, and Third Parties

Upbell does not require a user account, display third-party advertisements, or use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

We do not collect or sell personal information through the App, and we do not share App data with advertisers or data brokers.

The App uses Apple system frameworks to provide its functionality. Your use of your Apple device and related operating-system services is also subject to Apple's applicable privacy practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored by Upbell remains on your device until it is removed through available App controls, by deleting the App, or through applicable device management and backup processes.

Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored application data from that device. Copies may remain in device backups managed by you or Apple according to the applicable backup settings.

9. Data Security

Upbell relies on iOS application sandboxing and device security protections for locally stored data. No method of storing information is completely risk-free, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

Upbell is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

11. International Users

Upbell may be available in multiple countries. Its core functionality operates locally on your device, and we do not operate a user-data collection server for the App.

You may have privacy rights under the laws applicable to your location. You can contact us regarding questions about those rights.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App, legal requirements, or our practices. When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date on this page.
